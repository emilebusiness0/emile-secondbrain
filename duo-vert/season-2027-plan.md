## 0. Off-season prep discipline — start now, don't wait for spring

**Explicit lesson learned, stated 2026-08-26:** the diagnosed root cause of 2026 being
a weak season (see [[duo-vert/revenue-growth-plan]] — only 5 jobs all summer) was that
Emile and Beckett had the whole winter/spring available to prepare (website, ads,
systems) but didn't start until the season was already underway, telling themselves
"it's not summer yet, we can't do anything." With ~2 weeks of active work left in the
2026 season (to mid-September, per [[duo-vert/employee-hiring-plan]]), the explicit
plan is to start executing on all of this — hiring, branding, storage unit, legal/tax
setup, the UGC ad strategy — as soon as the season winds down, not wait until next
spring. This is the operating principle behind why so much 2027 planning is already
happening in August 2026, and should be treated as the default going forward: prep
work is off-season work, not something to defer until it's "the season" again.

**Gate lifted further, 2026-08-28:** Emile clarified "as soon as the season winds down"
doesn't mean literally waiting for the last job of 2026 to be finished — he already has
increasing free time as the season tapers off and wants to start on 2027 prep with
whatever time is available now, not hold off for a clean end-of-season boundary. Treat
the month-by-month timeline in section 12 below as able to pull forward
opportunistically rather than gated on a specific date.

## 1. Meta ads: hire on-camera talent for UGC-style video ads

Plan to hire a woman to appear in short (~10-15s) UGC-style video ads for Meta —
on-site at a job, showing the finished clean/restored work, explaining what Duo Vert
does, offering a quick quote. Paid gig, terms to be worked out and explained to her
directly.

**Casting reasoning (Emile's, 2026-08-26):** most people who engage with/click on the
current ads skew male. Hypothesis is that casting conventionally attractive women as
the on-camera talent will boost engagement and click-through on the ads specifically
because of that male-skewed audience, translating to more leads. This is a deliberate
creative/casting strategy for the ad talent role, not a hiring-plan detail — separate
from the [[duo-vert/employee-hiring-plan|sales reps and cleaners]] being hired for
actual fieldwork.

**Not yet decided:** pay rate/terms for the talent, how many videos, shooting cadence
(one-off vs. ongoing content pipeline), whether this replaces or supplements the
existing Meta ad creative that's already converting at ~2 leads/day (see
[[duo-vert/revenue-growth-plan]]).

**Recommended test methodology (given 2026-08-26):** the tactic itself (attractive
on-camera talent boosting ad attention) is a well-established, low-risk marketing
lever, not novel, but the real risk is optimizing for the wrong signal — an
attractive host can inflate engagement (views, likes, watch time, CTR) via
low-intent viewers without inflating what actually pays (booked quotes), and
Meta's algorithm will chase whichever signal it sees working, potentially skewing
delivery toward high-engagement/low-intent audiences over time. Recommendation:
run this as a genuine A/B test against the current control ad (the one already
converting at ~2 leads/day) and judge success on **cost-per-lead and
cost-per-booked-quote, not CTR or view count**, before committing to a full content
pipeline built around this talent.

- Team bonding events floated: a group trip to a water park, or a 2-day team trip

**Not yet decided:** budget for events/bonuses/comfort items, how this factors into

was included in the 22%/24%/54% payout scenarios there — treat as an added cost on

## 5. Legal/credibility prep: RBQ-type certification + formal tax registration

Two related legal-readiness items raised 2026-08-26, both framed as "get this actually
set up before next season" rather than "worth a conversation someday":

**Website design goal tied to this (2026-08-26):** Emile shared a screenshot of a
competitor site (renovatech.ca) that has a "trust bar" across the very top of every
page — phone number, an "RBQ Licensed #XXXX-XXXX-XX" badge, and a "under 24-hour
response" badge, all before the hero section. Wants the same pattern on duovert.ca
once Duo Vert has its own RBQ number: phone + RBQ license badge + a response-time
badge based on their own actual practice. Simple, concrete design target — revisit
with [[duo-vert/design-system]] once the license number itself exists (see above,
this is blocked on actually getting the 2.7 license, not a design question on its
own).

- **Trade certification/license — RESEARCHED 2026-08-26.** Confirmed via the RBQ's
  official "Liste des sous-catégories de licence" (rbq.gouv.qc.ca/sous-categories,
  doc 1592): the relevant category is **sous-catégorie 2.7 "Travaux d'emplacement"**
  (Annexe III, catégorie Entrepreneur spécialisé). Its exact scope: "la préparation
  et la finition d'emplacements, tels l'alignement, le nivellement, les clôtures, la
  démolition, le pavage et l'asphaltage, **le pavé imbriqué** ainsi que les travaux
  de construction similaires ou connexes" — this explicitly names pavé imbriqué
  (interlock pavers), so it's the right subcategory if Duo Vert pursues an RBQ
  license. Practical upside: Annexe III subcategories (unlike Annexe I/II) do not
  require passing a competency-in-construction exam, only the administrative/
  qualification requirements — comparatively lower barrier than a full Entrepreneur
  général license.
  **Open legal question, not yet resolved — needs a direct call to the RBQ (1 800
  361-0761) before deciding to pursue this:** general sources (ACQC, CAA-Québec)
  describe a **$20,000 exemption threshold** for repair/maintenance/renovation work
  ("travaux de réparation, d'entretien ou de rénovation évalués à moins de
  20 000 $") on rental/condo properties, which would put every Duo Vert job
  ($600-4,000, see [[duo-vert/company]]) well under the line. However, the official
  RBQ page on "who needs a licence" frames the requirement around anyone doing
  construction work *for another person* for payment/benefit — it's genuinely
  unclear from public sources whether that $20,000 exemption applies to paid
  contractors like Duo Vert or only to property owners doing DIY work themselves.
  **This distinction matters a lot:** if the exemption doesn't apply to contractors,
  Duo Vert may already be required to hold a 2.7 license regardless of job size;
  if it does apply, getting licensed becomes a pure credibility/trust play (the
  original motivation) rather than a compliance requirement. Confirm directly with
  RBQ before either assuming "we're exempt" or spending time/money on licensing.

  **Cost — RESEARCHED 2026-08-26, official RBQ fee schedule
  (rbq.gouv.qc.ca/licence/tarification-et-amendes/tarification-pour-lentrepreneur-de-construction):**
  a new "Spécialisé (annexe II et III)" license costs **$849.41** total (droits +
  frais) to apply — genuinely under $1,000, and confirmed **no competency-exam fee
  applies** (the $877.49/subcategory "dossier professionnel" exam fee is only for
  Annexe I/II subcategories; 2.7 is Annexe III and skips it, matching the
  no-exam-required point above). Annual renewal after that is $547.53. **Not
  included in that RBQ fee and not yet confirmed with an official source:** RBQ
  contractor licenses generally also require a **cautionnement (surety bond)**
  bought through a private insurer, separate from the RBQ fee — third-party
  training-course sites estimate this around $500-800/year, but that number is
  unverified against an official source and those sites have an incentive to
  upsell paid prep courses, so their broader "$1,800-3,200 first year" estimate
  should be treated with skepticism — it's not what the RBQ itself charges. Confirm
  the actual bond requirement/cost directly with RBQ or an insurance broker before
  budgeting.

  **Other trust-building "formations" looked at, since a formal RBQ license isn't
  the only credibility option:**
  - **Techo-Bloc "Techo-Pro"** — already known to be a free program (see
    [[project-current-todo-list]] item 8 / [[duo-vert/backlink-campaign]]), not a
    paid course. Competitor Paysagiste Leblanc is already listed; two eligibility
    questions still need asking before applying. This is a directory/certification
    listing, not classroom training.
  - **Techniseal certified applicator program** — Duo Vert already uses Techniseal
    sealant (see [[duo-vert/company]]), so this would be a natural-fit
    certification, but no public pricing was found; needs a direct inquiry to
    Techniseal to get real cost and requirements.
  - **ICPI (Interlocking Concrete Pavement Institute) Concrete Paver Installer
    course** — a real industry certification (2-day in-person course, run in
    Canada too), but no Quebec-specific pricing surfaced in research. Needs a
    direct check on educate.icpi.org for cost and nearest session before assuming
    it fits the sub-$1,000 budget.
  **Bottom line so far:** the RBQ 2.7 license application itself ($849.41,
  no exam) is the one option confirmed under $1,000. The manufacturer-specific
  certifications (Techniseal, ICPI) are plausible but need direct pricing
  confirmation before comparing them on cost.
- **Formal tax registration / invoicing.** Duo Vert currently has no GST/QST tax
  registration and no formal invoicing system — payment is Interac/cash only (see
  [[duo-vert/company]], confirmed 2026-08-21, first surfaced when a B2B client asked
  for a real facture). Previously flagged as "worth a conversation if it keeps coming
  up" — now upgraded to an actual 2027 prep priority: get taxes and proper invoicing
  set up during the off-season rather than reacting to it mid-job again.

order, because he was feeling overwhelmed by the volume of open items and wanted to
know what to do *when*. This is a rough skeleton, deliberately flexible — Emile said
he'd rather take the time to get things right than rush, so treat slippage of a few
weeks per phase as normal, not a sign of falling behind.

- **September 2026:** finish the last stretch of the 2026 season. Start informal,
  no-commitment-yet recruiting conversations with friends being considered for sales
  rep / cleaner roles (longest lead-time item, start earliest). Once the season ends,
  text the soccer coach to lock a discovery-call date (see
  [[personal/soccer-coach-website]]). Start batch-drafting GBP posts opportunistically.
- **October–November 2026 (main deep-work window):** write the training
  materials/SOPs for cleaners and sales reps — the biggest self-contained task, no
  dependencies. Have the soccer coach discovery call and start that build if timing
  allows. Spec out the GHL setup (pipeline stages, custom fields, contract template

  also the window flagged for the English site translation (section 9 above).

  start locking summer plans around now). Finish training materials if not already
  done. Sort out the storage unit (pricing, year-round vs. seasonal). Make sure the
  legal/tax handoff conversation with Beckett's dad is actually moving forward.
- **February–March 2027:** lock recruiting commitments for real. Finish any loose ends
  on training materials. Treat this window partly as buffer for whatever slipped from
  earlier months.
- **April 2027:** the readiness deadline itself — run the "if an employee showed up
  today, are we ready" test for real (see section 11 above), fix whatever gaps it
  finds. Order equipment (per-cleaner pressure washers), confirm the storage unit is
  ready. Finalize written pay agreements with Beckett's dad's legal input.
- **May 2027:** GHL access opens (see [[duo-vert/sheets-tracking]] for why access
  starts here, not earlier) — configure it using the spec built in October/November,
  migrate active leads out of the Sheet into GHL, final staging before season start.
