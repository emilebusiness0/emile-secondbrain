Duo Vert is [[personal/about-emile|Emile]]'s own company — a 2-person interlock paver (pavé-uni) business run with a partner, Beckett, based in Gatineau, Quebec.

**Services:** restoration/repair, cleaning, sealing (scellant — Techniseal brand), and leveling (nivelage) of interlocking paver surfaces (driveways, patios, walkways). Nivelage/nettoyage are not sold standalone — they're steps within a restoration project.

**How they operate:** always Emile & Beckett personally on-site, no subcontracting. Pressure washing at 3600 PSI / 2.5 GPM, Honda industrial engine. Protect lawn/plants before starting, full cleanup after. Optimal season June–September (possible window May–October). Polymeric sand lasts ~5 years; pavers themselves last 25–50 years.

**Pricing:** typical project runs $600–$4,000 depending on scope.

**Market benchmark for restoration (clean + re-sand + seal, no paver replacement), confirmed 2026-08-19:** competitor/market rate is ~$3/sq ft all-in (Quebec sources: cleaning+polymeric sand $1.25–$2/sq ft, sealing $1.20–$1.50/sq ft separately). Emile flagged that a higher $8–15/sq ft figure found in initial research was likely inflated/conflated with heavier repair work — the $3/sq ft figure is what he believes the actual market standard is, and research confirmed it. Useful as a competitor-pricing reference point for future quotes, not necessarily Duo Vert's own rate.

**Payment/materials model (confirmed 2026-08-18):** the quoted price is for labor/service only, never includes materials. Materials are bought by Emile/Beckett as needed throughout the job and billed to the client at cost, no markup — a pass-through, not something that reduces profit. Payment is typically 50% deposit before the job starts, then the remaining 50% of the service price plus the materials cost as one combined final payment after. See [[duo-vert/sheets-tracking]] for how this is tracked.

**Business registration clarified (2026-08-27):** Duo Vert is registered/licensed as a
business (Emile's words: "we are licensed as a business," confirmed on LinkedIn as
"fully licensed" since age 16) — this is separate from the RBQ 2.7 trade contractor
license discussed in [[duo-vert/season-2027-plan]], which remains unpursued/unresolved.
Don't conflate the two: "licensed" on its own (e.g. on LinkedIn or in casual
conversation) means the basic business registration, not an RBQ contractor's license.

**No formal invoicing yet (confirmed 2026-08-21):** Duo Vert takes payment by Interac e-transfer or cash only — no official invoice/receipt system, implying no GST/QST tax registration in place. Came up when a B2B lead (Gestion Walid Issawy Inc) asked for a facture made out to his company after accepting a quote. Workaround: offer a written confirmation (client's company name, project address, amount) for their records, without framing it as a formal accounting invoice. **Update 2026-08-26:** upgraded from "worth a conversation" to an actual 2027 off-season prep priority — see [[duo-vert/season-2027-plan]] for the tax-registration/invoicing plan, alongside a possible RBQ-type trade certification for credibility.
