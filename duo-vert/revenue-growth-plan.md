Context: one client all summer 2026 despite peak season, real financial pressure — see
[[duo-vert/backlink-campaign]] for the original diagnosis. On 2026-08-12, ran a 5-advisor LLM
council (Contrarian, First Principles, Expansionist, Outsider, Executor) specifically on "what
should Emile actually do, prioritized, with 6-8 weeks of season left."

## Council verdict, condensed

**Agreed unanimously:** unblock and launch the Google Ads campaign that day (was sitting built
but idle on Beckett's payment card — see [[duo-vert/google-ads-campaign]]). **Real, unresolved
tension the council surfaced:** is demand actually the bottleneck, or is it capacity (can 2
people even deliver more jobs in 6 weeks)? Never fully resolved — worth revisiting before
scaling any lead channel further, i.e. do the math on how many $600-4,000 jobs 2 people can
physically deliver by end of September before pushing harder on lead volume.

**Blind spot the council caught in peer review, not from any single advisor:** nobody proposed
deposits/progress payments to pull cash forward from the pipeline that already exists — flagged
as the fastest real cash move available, still not actioned as of 2026-08-13.

**The actual 5-item priority list that came out of this and got worked on in the days after:**
1. Fix GA4 tracking (was broken, unrelated discovery — see [[duo-vert/website-build-overview]])
   before spending more on Ads
2. Launch Google Ads
3. Call the one client from this summer for a referral/review ask
4. Targeted door-hanger drop (not random) — see [[duo-vert/print-collateral]] for the
   neighborhood-scouting method and confirmed streets
5. Start the Google LSA ("Google Guaranteed") application — long lead time, pure desk work,
   worth starting early even though it won't help this exact window

**Still open as of 2026-08-13:** items 2 (Ads launch status unconfirmed), 3 (client
referral call not confirmed done), 5 (LSA application not started). Item 1 done and verified.
Item 4 in progress — hangers arrive 2026-08-18, route planned, not yet executed.
