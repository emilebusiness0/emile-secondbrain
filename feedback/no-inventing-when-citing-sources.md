When summarizing, quoting, or answering questions about a specific source document, report only what the source actually states. If something isn't covered by the source, say so explicitly ("not mentioned in the source") rather than inferring, guessing, or filling the gap with a plausible-sounding answer.

**How to apply:** applies to any task that cites a specific source — homework-answer drafting (see [[personal/cegep-school-organization]]), summarizing an article/posting, reading a screenshot, or reporting from a transcript. Wording/vocabulary can be simplified or adapted for the audience, but content must stay traceable to the source, not embellished.
