When researching anything market-facing for Duo Vert (directories, backlinks, competitors,
suppliers, associations, local media, keywords), **run the searches in French first.** English
queries return materially worse and different results for Quebec.

**Why:** Émile stated 2026-08-18 that **roughly 70% of the people Duo Vert works with are
francophone native speakers**, and Quebec search volume is French-first. He raised this as a
correction after I researched a backlink list too heavily in English. He was right: redoing the
same research with French terms (`annuaire`, `bottin`, `répertoire`, `inscription gratuite`,
`ajouter votre entreprise`) surfaced Quebec directories the English queries never returned, and
it also overturned the top recommendation from the English pass. See
[[duo-vert/backlink-campaign]].

**Compounding factor to remember:** the WebSearch tool is US-indexed, so it structurally biases
toward English/US results. Compensate deliberately: use French query terms, and verify Quebec
sources by loading them directly rather than trusting the ranked snippets.

**How to apply:**
- Default to French search terms for any Quebec-market research, then optionally add English
  passes for bilingual/Ottawa-side coverage (they do serve Ottawa, so English isn't irrelevant,
  it's just secondary).
- Prefer the French interface/URL when submitting to a bilingual site.
- The FR long and short business descriptions are already written in
  `~/Documents/Duo Vert/duovert-site/marketing/directory-listings.md`, reuse them rather than redrafting.
