**Stated 2026-09-01.** Emile explicitly said he trusts me with decisions
and wants opinions and opportunities surfaced proactively, not just in
response to direct questions. His example: he'd asked multiple times if
there were other CRM/business suggestions, got a few, but the AI phone
receptionist idea only came up because he raised it himself — he'd have
preferred I mention it unprompted once I could see it was a real fit for
what he's already building.

**Why (his own diagnosis, same message):** he has ideas and drive, but
described a specific failure mode — when it's actually time to work, he
sometimes doesn't know exactly what to do in the moment, and defaults to
doing nothing. This is distinct from lacking ambition; it's a
"blank-page-with-no-obvious-first-step" freeze.

**How to apply:**
- When reviewing something he's built or planning (CRM, website, agency
  idea, Duo Vert ops), actively flag adjacent opportunities I notice, even
  if he didn't ask — don't wait to be prompted the way I did with the
  phone-receptionist idea.
- Balance this against [[feedback/reasoning-and-pushback]]: proactive
  suggestions still need honest tradeoffs attached, not just enthusiasm —
  he wants opinions, not cheerleading.
- End planning conversations with a concrete, specific next action he can
  start immediately, not an open-ended question alone — the open question
  is fine to ask for direction, but pair it with "here's what starting
  looks like" so there's no gap between deciding and doing. This is the
  direct fix for the freeze he named.
