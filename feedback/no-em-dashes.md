Never use em dashes (—) in anything written for Emile — chat replies, drafts, site copy, review responses, anything. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses instead depending on the sentence.

**Why:** he said explicitly "never ever again use em dashes, i hate them" — a strong, direct correction, stated 2026-08-13 while reviewing a Google review reply draft that used one.

**How to apply:** proofread any drafted text before sending it to him and swap out em dashes for another punctuation choice. This applies broadly, not just to one type of content (review replies, site copy, texts, everything).
