Emile asked (2026-08-26) for this to become an ongoing habit, not a one-off cleanup: every time a file gets touched or created during work together, it should land in the right place immediately — he doesn't want to have to ask for organizing passes like the 2026-08-26 one (see [[personal/mac-file-organization]], [[personal/cegep-school-organization]]).

**The mental model he described** (using his existing setup as the reference examples):
- **Top-level "domain" folders** are the big, independent areas of his life — currently `Documents/Cegep/` (school), `Documents/Duo Vert/` (his paver business), `Documents/emile-secondbrain/` (the vault itself). Each one is self-contained and holds everything related to that domain.
- **Subfolders inside a domain** are the sections within it — e.g. inside `Duo Vert/`: `Admin/`, `Assets/`, `Finances/`, `soumissions/`, the site repos; inside `Cegep/`: one folder per course.
- A **new top-level domain folder** is warranted when something is a genuinely new, independent area of his life — comparable in scope to "my business" or "school" — not just a new topic within an existing one.
- A **new subfolder** is warranted when something is a new project/topic within an existing domain (his example: a new business initiative under Duo Vert would just be a new subfolder there, not a new top-level folder).

**Why:** he wants to open Documents and immediately know where anything lives — one folder per real-life domain, sections inside it — with nothing loose at the top level and no need to hunt across scattered folders for related material.

**How to apply going forward:**
- Whenever a file is created, downloaded, or otherwise touched during a task, place it in the correct existing folder/subfolder without being asked.
- If it doesn't fit anywhere that exists yet, decide: is this a new *domain* (top-level `Documents/<Domain>/`) or a new *subfolder* within an existing domain? Use the scope test above — "as big as school or the business" → new top-level folder; "a new thing within school or the business" → new subfolder.
- **If it's not clear which one applies, ask him rather than guessing** — he said this explicitly, don't silently pick one.
- He's trying to name files sensibly on his end specifically to make this classification easier — lean on filenames/content together, not just one or the other (see the [[personal/cegep-school-organization]] correction: content topic alone isn't reliable, since e.g. a prof can assign legal-topic homework inside a management course).

**Anticipated near-term:** Emile said he's about to start "website conception" soon — this is his planned freelance web-design business for clients other than Duo Vert (see [[personal/website-build-playbook]], first noted 2026-08-05, no agency name yet). When that actually starts, expect it to warrant its own new top-level `Documents/<name>/` domain folder (sibling of `Duo Vert/` and `Cegep/`), not a subfolder inside `Duo Vert/` — it's a distinct business, not a Duo Vert initiative. Ask him what to name the folder when it actually kicks off rather than guessing "website conception" as the literal name.
