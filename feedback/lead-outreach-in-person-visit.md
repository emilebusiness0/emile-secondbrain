When drafting outreach messages (email/SMS) to new [[duo-vert/company|Duo Vert]] leads, don't propose a phone call or ask for photos as an intermediate step — go straight to proposing an in-person visit date to evaluate the job.

**Why:** Emile corrected this on the Jean Leclerc lead (2026-08-10) — "arrete de demander de faire ca en ligne, il faut juste planifier une date pour aller la bas." His workflow for quotes is in-person visits, not remote qualification.

**How to apply:** every future lead-outreach draft (email or SMS) should ask directly for a day/time that works for a site visit — skip "can we hop on a call" or "send me some photos" framing.
