When quoting or corresponding with a Duo Vert client, don't state exact site/paver measurements (square footage, dimensions) unprompted. If a client directly asks for the measurement, share it.

See also: [[duo-vert/soumission-template]], [[duo-vert/employee-hiring-plan]] (sqft-based pay estimates, an internal, not client-facing, use of measurements)
