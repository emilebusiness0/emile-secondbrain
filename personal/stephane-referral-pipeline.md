**Background.** Before summer 2026, Emile's dad introduced him to Stephane,
who works for a bigger-scope business consulting/organizing agency for
companies. That agency's clients often need websites redone, but the
agency doesn't do web work itself - it refers clients out to web builders.
Emile's dad showed Stephane the Duo Vert site (which Emile built himself),
Stephane liked it, and asked to meet - a Zoom call happened before summer
where Stephane raised one specific client need (a real estate business)
and floated "maybe we can partner sometime."

**Timeline:** Zoom call before summer 2026 → Emile followed up once or
twice over the summer, no response → followed up again 2026-09-01,
Stephane confirmed he hadn't forgotten, delay was his and the client's
vacations. **Real estate client's info expected by Friday (2026-09-04).**
Emile's part is design/assembly, not content creation - Stephane's side
has already prepared the content, which is a real efficiency compared to
a from-scratch discovery process like [[personal/soccer-coach-website]].

**Second lead, less certain:** Stephane has a brother who runs two
separate businesses, both reportedly needing their sites redone.
Described by Emile as "probable" but not confirmed - don't treat as
committed work until it actually firms up.

**This is a genuinely different channel than the soccer coach lead** -
a warm referral through a business intermediary, not a personal favor.
Two things flagged as needing direct clarification with Stephane, not
assumed:
1. Whether Stephane expects a referral fee/commission or formal
   partnership structure for sending work - common in this kind of
   intermediary relationship, worth asking plainly rather than guessing.
2. Pricing should be real market rate here (see the tier framework in
   [[personal/agency-idea]]), not a friend discount - this is a
   professional relationship where underpricing doesn't build the same
   trust it does with a close friend, and Stephane is the one person who
   could send more referral work.

**Capacity risk, named directly (2026-09-01):** if the real estate site,
both of the brother's businesses, and the soccer coach's site all land
around the same time, that's up to four solo client projects at once for
a first-timer. Recommended sequencing rather than parallel commitment:
real estate site first (nearest deadline, most concrete), the rest only
once actually confirmed, not planned around while still "probable."

**Sequencing confirmed by Emile, same day:** the real estate client is now
the actual first agency project - ahead of the soccer coach's site, which
Emile downgraded to "less urgent, mostly just gonna get discussed" for
now (see [[personal/soccer-coach-website]], discovery call already
requested, not yet scheduled). The brother's two businesses are also
being treated as lower urgency/uncertain, matching the "probable, not
committed" framing above.

**Pipeline status update (2026-09-02), Emile's own estimate, not
independently verified:** three real prospects now in motion, each
scoped to a website only for now ("I don't know about other features" -
CRM/ads not yet part of any of these conversations). One "guaranteed"
(most likely the real estate client, info expected 2026-09-04 per above).
One at "maybe 95%" (not yet identified precisely in this conversation -
could be one of the brother's two businesses, or a separate lead;
identity worth confirming next time it comes up rather than assumed).
One "owns two different businesses," at "maybe 80%" (most likely the
brother described above, still two separate sites needing redo).
Treat these percentages as Emile's gut read, not firm commitments -
consistent with the existing "probable, not committed" framing already
in this file.

**Confirmed as the highest-value channel he has (2026-09-02):** research
pulled into [[personal/agency-idea]] the same day found referral
partnerships generate 31% of agency revenue from only ~10% of pipeline
and convert 3x higher than cold outreach - this Stephane channel matches
that exact pattern, not just a nice-to-have lead source.
