**Stated 2026-09-01.** Emile is 17, turning 18 in roughly 5-6 months
(so around early-mid 2027). Framed his ambition explicitly around this
timeline — wants to build momentum now rather than wait, citing that
"the sooner the better" even though he knows some people succeed starting
later.

**The actual goal, in his own words:** not about being "rich" — about
being able to afford time and travel with his mom, who dreams of
traveling the world. Financial freedom is the means, not the end. Useful
framing for how to pitch tradeoffs to him: speed/hustle framed around
time-with-family lands better than generic wealth framing.

**Personal turnaround, disclosed candidly:** the past year followed a
breakup with his girlfriend. Before that, he describes the relationship
period as having "horrible routines" — poor eating, and he specifically
named a porn addiction he says he's worked on but not "100% changed."
Since the breakup he says he started going to the gym, put real
importance on his faith (God), stopped wasting time daily, and got
ambitious about building his businesses (Duo Vert, now the agency idea).
He connects his current drive directly to this turnaround, not just
generic ambition.

**How to use this:** when advising on pacing/workload for the agency idea
or Duo Vert, this is real personal stakes for him, not just a business
experiment — but it's also exactly the kind of high-energy, high-conviction
moment where honest pushback matters more, not less (per
[[feedback/reasoning-and-pushback]]). Don't mirror the emotional intensity
back uncritically; keep giving grounded, testable next steps rather than
validating the scale of ambition itself. Faith/family motivation can be
acknowledged briefly and respectfully but the working relationship should
stay focused on concrete execution, per his own stated pattern of getting
"pumped" and needing follow-through structure to avoid dopamine-spike-then-
nothing cycles (he named this risk himself).

**Self-comparison to Beckett (2026-09-01, same day):** Emile described
himself as more ambitious/expansion-driven than Beckett, his Duo Vert
co-founder — his read is that Beckett prefers to keep working what's
already known rather than keep expanding into new things, and Emile sees
this as a real difference in what drives them, not a knock on Beckett (he
was careful to say he loves him and rates him highly). He also named a
specific self-image: expects to be "that guy" interviewed one day about
having pushed further than others, citing the trope of people who
interview millionaires/billionaires on TikTok as the kind of story he
sees himself in.

Response given, not just validated: reframed the Beckett gap as a
plausible complementary division-of-labor question (one person optimizing
depth/stability, one optimizing growth/expansion — both real skills,
relevant to the existing 2027 role split in
[[duo-vert/season-2027-plan]]), not a drive scoreboard. Also pushed back
on the "guy who gets interviewed at 30" self-image as a motivating story
worth holding loosely rather than treating as evidence — flagged it as
the kind of narrative that can substitute for actual weekly output if he's
not careful, consistent with the dopamine-spike risk he already named
himself.

working through the agency idea's marketing plan (see
[[personal/agency-idea]]), Emile listed Instagram, an agency website,

mom and God proud. This is the dopamine-spike/overwhelm risk from the
entry above showing up live, not hypothetical - useful confirmation that
scope-creep-under-pressure is a real, recurring pattern for him, not a
one-off.

Response given: named it directly rather than smoothing over it, cut the
list back to the one real next action in front of him (finishing the
real estate client's site, info due 2026-09-04), and pointed out
everything else on his list is explicitly backlogged already in
[[personal/agency-idea]] pending a finished first project - reinforced
that wanting the full five-channel marketing stack figured out this week
is the perfectionism/overwhelm pattern itself, not a planning gap. Faith/
family motivation acknowledged briefly, not dismissed, but redirected to
concrete execution per the standing guidance above.
